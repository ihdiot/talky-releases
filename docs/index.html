How it works

Three moves. That's the whole app.

Talky sits in your system tray and waits. It works anywhere you can type — Discord, Docs, game chat, code editors, browser boxes.

01

Tap the hotkey

One tap and Talky starts listening. No holding anything down — ramble as long as you want.

Ctrl+Win
02

Say the thing

Whisper — the same class of AI the big cloud services use — transcribes on your CPU or GPU, and your words type out live every time you pause.

03

Tap again — done

In a text box, everything lands at your cursor with smart punctuation and your personal dictionary. Nothing focused? It's captured and ready to paste.

Privacy

Your voice never leaves your machine.

That's not a marketing line — it's the architecture. The speech model runs locally, so there is simply no server for your audio to go to.

Stays on your PC

  • Every word you speak — transcribed locally by Whisper
  • Everything Talky types, your history, your stats
  • Your personal dictionary and settings
  • Works with the network cable unplugged

The only things that touch the internet

  • First launch downloads the speech model (skip that with the Portable or Mac zip — the model's already inside)
  • An optional, off-by-default check for new versions
  • That's the whole list. No telemetry, no analytics, no account.

FAQ

Quick answers

What do I need to run it?
Windows 10 or newer — or an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 and up) — plus a microphone. On Windows an NVIDIA GPU makes transcription near-instant with the big model; on CPU-only machines Talky picks a lighter model — still solid, just a bit slower. M-series Macs handle it comfortably on the chip alone.
Is it free?
$1. That’s it. One-time, no subscription — you buy it once and mumble forever. The builds on this page are how you get it onto Windows or a Mac.
Mac version?
Live now — alpha, Apple Silicon (M1 and newer). One thing to know up front: on Mac right now, Talky copies each dictation to your clipboard. To drop it in, either press ⌘V (hold the Command key ⌘ and tap V) or right-click → Paste (Control-click works too) wherever you want it. On Windows it types straight at your cursor; cursor-typing on Mac is coming. Grab the Mac zip from the downloads above: unzip, double-click Talky.app, and allow microphone access when macOS asks. It's not signed yet, so the first open may be blocked — sometimes silently, so if nothing happens: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway" right after you double-click (on older macOS, right-click → Open works too). One time only. Intel Macs aren't supported. Rough edges go in an issue on the releases repo.
Does it auto-send my messages?
Never. Talky types (or copies) your words and stops. Pressing Enter is always your move — by design, there's no auto-send in the app at all.
I found a bug. Where do I yell?
Open an issue on the releases repo. For the gnarly ones, run talky.exe --bundle-logs and drop the zip it makes in your report — it's redacted (no transcripts), just diagnostics.
Who makes this?
IHDIOT — one guy who got tired of cloud dictation apps phoning home. Talky is built in the open with its community of testers.
